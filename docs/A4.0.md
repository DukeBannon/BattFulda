# A4.0: Map-first visual foundation

A4.0 begins translating the approved final-product concept into the real
Amiga 1200 runtime. It is a layout and information-hierarchy milestone, not the
final terrain-art pass.

## Visual direction

The interface follows the approved design reference in
`docs/design/battalion_fulda_visual_target.png`:

- the battlefield is the primary visual surface;
- a narrow left panel presents the selected unit or current map location;
- a restrained title strip identifies the game and milestone;
- a compact command strip keeps the controls visible without covering terrain;
- 500-meter square cells remain the simulation grid;
- graphics will use original AGA pixel art rather than copied game assets.

John Tiller-style information hierarchy is the main structural influence, with
the direct map readability of Steel Panthers as a secondary influence. The
reference image is an aspiration for composition, density, and atmosphere; it
is not a promise that every pictured feature already exists in A4.0.

## Runtime changes

- The visible map grows from 20 by 11 cells to 29 by 13 cells.
- The tactical inspector moves from the right side to a 160-pixel left column.
- Selected-unit identity, formation, strength, morale, suppression, readiness,
  movement, elevation, and compound terrain remain available.
- With no unit selected, the panel shows compound terrain, elevation, and grid
  coordinates.
- Mouse selection, right-click cancellation, WASD/Enter/Escape controls, fast
  raster scrolling, and mouse edge-panning retain their existing behavior.
- Visible updates are composed in an off-screen bitmap and presented at the
  display refresh, preventing newly exposed rows and columns from being seen
  drawing cell-by-cell.

## Acceptance

```powershell
cd C:\Repositories\Games\BattFulda
.\build-amiga.ps1 -Clean -Test -Run
```

1. Confirm the map occupies most of the display and shows 29 columns by 13
   rows.
2. Confirm the title strip, left tactical panel, and bottom command strip are
   legible and do not overlap the map.
3. Move across and pan through the map with both mouse and WASD.
   Newly exposed rows and columns should appear complete rather than painting
   progressively across the visible display.
4. Select NATO and Warsaw Pact units and confirm their database-backed details
   fit within the left panel.
5. Right-click or press Escape to cancel a selection, then press Escape again
   to exit.
