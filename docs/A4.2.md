# A4.2: Platform-decision visual slice

A4.2 is the visual go/no-go milestone for the stock Amiga 1200 target. It must
show the intended final visual language in the real game rather than asking the
player to extrapolate from engineering graphics or concept art.

## Required presentation

- Final map-first composition at 640 by 256 pixels
- Purpose-built compact bitmap typography
- Authored terrain tiles for representative Central European clear, cultivated,
  wooded, rough, marsh, water, and built-up ground
- Layered roads, waterways, bridges, railways, the former inner-German border,
  place labels, and elevation treatment
- Finished NATO and Warsaw Pact platoon/team counters with category symbols,
  side identity, selection state, and readable stacking treatment
- Selection and representative movement/reach overlays
- Finished left information panel and bottom command strip
- A functional overview map showing the viewport and unit presence
- Smooth mouse and keyboard cursor movement and camera scrolling

## Technical boundary

The baseline remains a stock A1200 with a 68020, AGA, and 2 MB Chip RAM. The
proven standard 640 by 256 high-resolution display uses 16 simultaneous colors.
The game may change palette registers by screen region later, but A4.2 must not
depend on an enhanced emulator-only graphics mode.

The complete 40 by 40 landscape is cached once in Chip RAM. Camera movement is
then a hardware-assisted copy of the visible rectangle, followed by dynamic
units, overlays, and cursor drawing. This avoids both progressive repainting
and the delay of regenerating hundreds of terrain cells per camera step.

## Acceptance decision

A4.2 succeeds only if the emulator output is attractive, legible, responsive,
and recognizably close in spirit to the approved design reference. If it does
not meet that standard after focused art iteration, the project should evaluate
a Windows target rather than continue on the assumption that later polish will
solve the platform gap.
