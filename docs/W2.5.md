# W2.5: Spotting and fog of war

## Status: initial playable scope accepted, 2026-09-17

User playtesting accepted the W2.5 spotting/fog-of-war deliverable. Remaining
adjacent-move usability and combat-balance reports are tracked in ROADMAP.md;
acceptance does not mean those issues are resolved or ratings validated.

## Contact-model foundation

Each side maintains its own immutable contact reports, separate from live enemy
units. Reports contain either an identified sighting or an uncertain cue with
an approximate location. Uncertain reports omit the enemy name and category.
After LOS is lost, a report remains at its last observed location and eventually
expires; hidden movement and casualties do not update it.

Five visibility presets are scaffolded: Open, Assisted, Standard, Veteran,
and Expert. Open reveals living enemy units. Other presets vary visual range,
obscured range, cue range, and report retention. These are provisional gameplay
values, not historical sensor specifications. They do not yet alter command,
communications, or order delays.

Movement and firing events are supplied explicitly by the caller. There is no
passive sound cue from a stationary, silent enemy. Optical detection uses the
same woods and elevation LOS as direct fire. Weather, daylight, optics, and
thermal-sensor distinctions are deferred.

## First playable integration

- Contacts refresh every five simulated seconds during WEGO, plus at fire
  resolution and turn completion. Actual position changes and fired shots
  produce short-lived cue events.
- Standard visibility is the default. Click OPTIONS to choose one of the five
  presets, or press F5 to cycle them in Planning or Review. Changing presets
  resets contact history, so Open is a debugging aid, not a competitive toggle.
- Current identified contacts use red class markers without live strength
  information. Uncertain contacts use gold question marks; stale markers use
  dashed borders. Clicking their hex displays the contact report, not a live
  enemy unit inspector. The minimap follows the same contact knowledge.
- Enemy routes and fire plans are hidden outside Open. Review totals report
  friendly shots and friendly losses, not hidden enemy casualties.
- Only current identified contacts are selectable direct-fire targets. Fire
  is held if identification is lost before resolution. Uncertain contacts
  cannot be targeted as exact units; area fire is not implemented.
- Opposing AI targets its own identified contacts. Without contact intelligence
  it advances toward a fixed scenario objective rather than a hidden NATO unit.

## Playtest

1. Restart the rebuilt game. Standard should initially hide distant enemies.
2. Advance units and Execute. Check that contact markers appear only when
   visually identified or supported by nearby movement/firing cues.
3. Select a contact's hex and inspect its report. Uncertain reports must not
   disclose a name, strength, or exact location. Stale markers must not follow
   hidden enemy movement.
4. Try Fire against a gold marker: it should not accept an exact-unit target.
5. Use OPTIONS / Open to compare with full visibility, then restore Standard.

## Remaining tuning and follow-up

Playtest fixes: clicking the actual drawn bounds of an animated/stack-offset
friendly counter selects that unit rather than the underlying hex. Contact
markers also have direct hit testing for their report. Movement animation now
interpolates along the same road-snapped points used by the plotted route;
hex occupancy and movement cost remain unchanged. Curved road segments between
snap points and route-finalization alignment still need visual playtesting.
Zoom currently spans 34–96 pixel hex widths. At minimum zoom on a large screen,
the finite map may be narrower than the viewport, leaving background visible.

Urban LOS now uses the conservative woods-style rule: the first urban target
hex is obscured, but any intervening urban hex blocks sight and direct fire,
regardless of elevation. Explicit street corridors and above-rooftop sight
are not modeled. The shared LOS rule applies to spotting and the overlay too.

Fire-order presentation: dashed colored arrows are pending orders; solid
colored arrows with a target ring represent actual firing results (not proof
of damage). Held/invalid orders are gray dotted lines without arrowheads,
with a source X and NO SHOT label, and no target impact ring.

Combat presentation backlog: weapon-appropriate firing flashes/tracer or
projectile travel, scaled impact effects distinguishing misses from damaging
hits, and weapon/impact sounds. These must obey fog of war: unseen effects
must not disclose exact hidden positions; audible cues use the contact model.
One resolution represents a platoon engagement, not necessarily a single shot.

DEFEND is currently a placeholder, with no order-specific combat benefit.
Proposed defensive posture: halt and deploy, improve cover/target exposure
where terrain permits, support engagement/hold-fire rules, and require time
for preparation or digging in. Hull-down positions and entrenchments need
explicit modeling, not a universal armor bonus. Details remain undecided.
ASSAULT is also a placeholder; W2.6 covers close combat and morale reactions.
Proposed assault behavior combines approach, close-range engagement and
contesting the occupied hex, with suppression/morale influencing success and
retreat. These are future rules, not current functionality.

Provisional stacking capacity is four footprint points: command elements and
anti-tank teams cost one; other units cost two. Two maneuver platoons can share
or pass through a hex. An already full hex waits or uses a short local bypass;
independent over-capacity transit remains deferred. Formation-size data will
be needed for larger formations and support units.

Stacked friendly counters are offset; repeated clicks on their hex or Enter
cycle selection. Capacity holds and enemy encounters give explicit feedback.
Route planning excludes only current identified enemy contact cells supplied
by the issuing side. Actual hidden enemy occupancy blocks execution, not
planning. Bypasses never inspect other hidden enemy positions.

Weapon corrections and remaining audit work are in UNIT_CAPABILITY_AUDIT.md.
Terrain artwork is unchanged.

Identification currently happens immediately when a unit meets the preset's
range and terrain-LOS conditions. Gradual acquisition, observer-specific
intelligence and sharing delays, detailed sound propagation, richer contact
classification, and difficulty effects on command/communications are not yet
implemented. Preset values require playtesting. Weather/daylight and sensor
profiles remain deferred beyond the accepted initial W2.5 scope.
