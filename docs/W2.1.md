# W2.1: Orders interface

W2.1 is the first interactive gameplay slice. It adds order plotting without
changing unit positions or resolving a WEGO turn.

## Player interaction

- Right-clicking a NATO unit selects it and opens an adjacent cascading action
  menu.
- The Move submenu offers Quick, Tactical, and Hunt postures.
- Left-click or Enter adds a numbered waypoint. The provisional route follows
  the cursor before the next waypoint is committed.
- Double-click or the Confirm footer button stores the order.
- Backspace or Undo removes the most recent waypoint.
- Escape, right-click, or Cancel abandons the draft without replacing an
  existing confirmed order.
- Cancel Orders removes the selected unit's confirmed plan.
- Confirmed plans remain visible as dashed blue routes; the route currently
  being plotted is solid yellow.
- Enemy units can be inspected but cannot receive player orders.

The Fire, Assault, and Defend entries deliberately identify their later
milestones instead of performing placeholder combat.

## Route boundary

W2.1 uses an unweighted six-neighbor hex route solely to support interface
feedback. W2.2 replaces this preview calculation with movement-class costs,
road advantages, crossing legality, bridges, streams, rivers, amphibious
capability, and A* pathfinding. Confirmed W2.1 orders therefore demonstrate the
planning workflow but do not yet promise a legal or optimal battlefield route.
Estimated waypoint arrival times and player-authored waypoint delays are logged
in `docs\ROADMAP.md` for consideration after the movement-cost model and WEGO
resolver are stable.

## Acceptance

1. A right-click action menu opens beside a selected friendly unit.
2. Move posture selection enters an unmistakable plotting state.
3. Mouse and keyboard waypoint placement produces a connected route preview.
4. Multiple waypoints are numbered and may be undone.
5. Confirmed plans persist independently for multiple units.
6. Draft cancellation does not move a unit or damage a prior confirmed plan.
7. The inspector reports order posture, status, and waypoint count.
8. Existing map selection, panning, minimap navigation, and zoom remain intact.
9. Database tests, validation, and the Windows build pass.
