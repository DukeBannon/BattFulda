# W1: 250 m battlefield foundation

W1 replaces the Windows proof map with a geographically aligned 250 m
flat-top hex battlefield covering the same 20 by 20 km Point Alpha corridor.
The regular hex geometry requires 92 columns by 80 rows, for 7,360 cells.

## Data model

- SQLite schema version 6 stores the map dimensions, projected origin, and
  geographic extent.
- `map_cell` stores base terrain, elevation, and compact six-direction feature
  masks for selection, minimap display, and future rules.
- `map_edge` stores the canonical connected road and waterway edges plus the
  crossing classification used for road-over-water bridges and future authored
  fords.
- `map_feature` and `map_feature_vertex` retain the original projected road and
  river paths plus short bridge decks derived at their true geographic
  intersections. The Windows renderer therefore does not force unrelated
  features onto the same center-to-center hex line.
- Cultivated ground is distinct from clear ground. DLM250 returns no explicit
  agriculture polygons in this clip, so otherwise unclassified rural ground
  is provisionally cultivated; `clear` remains available for later 1985 map
  editing.

The schema and renderer distinguish local, secondary, and primary roads. This
map clip contains local roads and the B84/B278 secondary roads but no motorway;
primary-road support remains ready for later maps. W1 records the classes but
does not assign movement time. W2 will provide database-authored traversal
times by road class, mobility class, weather, and movement posture.

## Runtime

The Windows game loads dimensions and geographic metadata from the exported
database data rather than hard-coding a 40 by 40 map. Roads and waterways are
drawn from preserved projected vertices; bridge decks are drawn last at their
derived intersections. Terrain is cached in visible 1,024-pixel chunks at each zoom level so
the 7,360-cell battlefield remains responsive.

The demonstration units were reprojected from their former 500 m cells to the
equivalent locations on the new grid. Their placement remains illustrative,
not a final historical order of battle.

## Acceptance

1. The inspector and minimap address a 92 by 80, 250 m map.
2. Cultivated, wooded, urban, and open-water terrain load from external data.
3. Roads and waterways follow their independent geographic paths.
4. Streams, minor rivers, and major rivers have distinct widths.
5. Bridge decks draw above water while ordinary roads draw below it.
6. Local and secondary roads in Point Alpha are reported distinctly, with the
   primary-road class supported for later maps.
7. NATO and Warsaw Pact units occupy their equivalent geographic positions.
8. Keyboard, edge, drag, minimap, and wheel navigation work across the map.
9. Scrolling reuses cached terrain chunks without progressive repainting.
10. Database validation, automated tests, and the Release build pass.
