# W2.3: WEGO movement execution

W2.3 turns the route planner into the first executable gameplay system. The
player plans movement for multiple NATO units and then resolves all confirmed
orders against the same 15-minute game clock.

## Delivered

- Execute Turn is available as a touch-friendly footer command.
- All confirmed movement orders begin together rather than resolving one unit
  at a time.
- Movement accrues in small internal time slices from each unit's database
  movement allowance and the W2.2 cost of every traversed edge.
- Quick, Tactical, and Hunt therefore produce different movement rates and may
  retain their different A* routes.
- Entering, leaving, or cutting across a road-bearing hex without using its
  connected road edge incurs a data-driven access cost. This keeps vehicle
  routes on continuous roads when an apparent cross-country shortcut offers
  only a marginal saving.
- Counters and minimap markers animate continuously between hex centers during
  playback.
- The header advances through the simulated turn time while the footer reports
  resolution progress and the number of units still moving.
- The scenario start date/time and turn duration are loaded from the database.
- Playback can be paused and resumed with the footer or Space.
- A unit that exhausts its turn allowance stops at its last occupied hex. Its
  remaining route and partial progress toward the next hex carry forward.
- Multiple friendly units cannot end a movement step in the same occupied hex.
  Contending units wait at the boundary and receive a visible traffic-hold
  marker and inspector explanation. After a short hold, a unit attempts a
  legal local bypass while preserving the original destination.
- Warsaw Pact formations receive basic tactical advance orders during each
  resolution. Their red routes, moving counters and minimap markers are
  visible, and the footer reports NATO and Pact movement separately.
- The command-button corridor suppresses bottom-edge panning, so moving the
  pointer toward Execute does not shift the battlefield.
- Movement that finishes early is marked complete. The turn enters Review
  before the player explicitly begins the next planning phase.
- The inspector reports executing, partial, and complete order states, movement
  cost spent during the turn, and traffic holds.

## Player workflow

1. Plan and confirm movement for one or more friendly units.
2. Select Execute in the footer.
3. Watch simultaneous movement, optionally pausing with Space or the footer.
4. Inspect the final positions and partial orders in Review.
5. Select Next Turn or press Enter to resume planning.

## Resolution model

Each unit receives `move_points * 10` cost units per scenario turn. A movement
edge consumes its W2.2 posture-specific terrain, road, slope, and crossing cost.
This gives the initial 250-meter battlefield plausible relative movement while
keeping every tuning value outside the animation code.

W2.3 never permits counters to stack. A waiting unit preserves accrued
progress and may enter as soon as the hex clears. If a stationary counter
continues to block the route and another legal path exists, the route is
rebuilt around current traffic. An occupied destination remains a hold rather
than silently changing the player's intended destination.

## Verification

Run:

```powershell
python .\tools\test_manage_database.py
.\build-windows.ps1 -Test
```

The movement checks cover legal water crossings and A* detours from W2.2 plus
partial-turn movement, next-turn progress retention, simultaneous
same-destination traffic contention, and automatic bypass of stationary
traffic.

  congestion resolution.
