# A4.1: AGA visual feasibility

A4.1 tests whether the approved visual direction can be achieved inside the
real stock Amiga 1200 runtime. It replaces the engineering-style flat marks
with a first controlled high-resolution art system while retaining the A4.0 map-first
layout and all working interaction.

## Display budget

- 640 by 256 high-resolution display
- 4 bitplanes and a controlled 16-color palette
- one visible bitmap and one off-screen bitmap
- one cached 640 by 640 pixel rendering of the complete Point Alpha map
- approximately 360 KB of Chip RAM for the display and map bitmaps
- 16 by 16 pixel reusable terrain cells

This leaves ample space within the 2 MB Chip RAM baseline for compiled map,
unit, formation, scenario, font, and future graphic data.

## First art system

- Clear and elevated ground receive restrained field and grass variation.
- Woods use three-tone clustered canopies instead of repeated letter-like
  marks.
- Rough ground, marsh, water, and settlements have distinct textures.
- Roads have dark shoulders and light center lines; rivers have shadow and
  highlight colors; bridge cells receive a separate deck mark.
- NATO and Warsaw Pact counters use distinct colored frames, a neutral center,
  and category symbols for command, tank, infantry, reconnaissance, anti-tank,
  artillery, air defense, engineer, and rocket-artillery units.
- Variation is derived deterministically from map coordinates, so it costs no
  additional map storage and never changes between runs.

## Acceptance

```powershell
cd C:\Repositories\Games\BattFulda
.\build-amiga.ps1 -Clean -Test -Run
```

1. Confirm the new terrain is more natural and remains immediately readable.
2. Confirm roads, waterways, woods, settlements, and bridges remain distinct
   while scrolling.
3. Confirm NATO and Warsaw Pact counters are distinguishable and their internal
   category symbols differ between unit types.
4. Confirm cursor and selected-unit outlines remain visible over every terrain
   color.
5. Pan horizontally and vertically with mouse and WASD and watch for flicker,
   progressive painting, or unacceptable delay.
6. Select units from both sides and confirm the database-backed information
   panel remains correct.

Acceptance of this pass establishes that the final mockup's visual language is
practical on the stock A1200. Later passes will replace procedural prototypes
with authored tile sets, add a compact bitmap font, and incorporate map labels,
railways, the former border, and a minimap.
