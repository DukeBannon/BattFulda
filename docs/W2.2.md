# W2.2: Data-driven route planning

W2.2 replaces the W2.1 interface-only route with the first authoritative
movement model. It plans legal routes but deliberately does not execute them;
WEGO movement resolution belongs to W2.3.

## Delivered

- A* pathfinding over the 92 by 80 Point Alpha hex grid.
- Mobility-specific movement profiles for foot, wheeled, tracked, towed, and
  mixed units.
- Separate Quick, Tactical, and Hunt costs.
- Terrain, road-class, elevation, water-crossing, bridge, and ford costs loaded
  from SQLite-generated CSV data rather than embedded in the renderer.
- Actual river-crossing boundaries derived from the geographic water lines.
  River-course links remain presentation data and are not mistaken for crossing
  barriers.
- Streams are normally fordable. Unbridged minor and major rivers block
  non-amphibious units; amphibious units may cross with a substantial penalty.
- Bridges permit normal ground movement. Open-water hexes remain impassable to
  the ground movement model.
- A road receives its movement advantage at a water boundary only when the
  crossing is actually classified as a bridge.
- Known enemy-occupied hexes are excluded from movement routes.
- Route cost and reachability appear in the inspector and footer. Blocked route
  previews and the destination cursor turn red, and blocked plans cannot be
  confirmed.

## Data additions

SQLite schema version 7 adds terrain, road, crossing, and general movement-cost
tables plus `map_crossing_edge`. The generated Point Alpha crossing layer is
stored in `data/maps/point_alpha_crossings.csv` and is reproducible with
`tools/build_movement_edges.py`.

The scenario order-of-battle export now includes each unit's mobility class and
movement allowance. This uses the amphibious data established earlier rather
than inferring capability from a counter name.

## Verification

Run the database and movement checks with:

```powershell
python .\tools\test_manage_database.py
.\build-windows.ps1 -Test
```

The focused movement checks use the real battlefield data to confirm stream,
river, amphibious, bridge, open-water, and legal-detour behavior.
